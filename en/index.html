Our foundation

Sustainably better software.

We stand for MEHR.WERT – and that starts with our approach. To us, sustainability means making conscious decisions and taking responsibility for long-term consequences: for people, systems and resources. Economic viability, maintainability and the ability to act belong together.

We consider AI where it offers concrete value – for example in analysis, documentation or quality assurance. Benefits, quality, costs and security guide its use. Professional judgment and responsibility remain with people.
